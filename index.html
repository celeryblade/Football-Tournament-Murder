At the height of the World Cup, the tournament's most celebrated player is discovered dead in a private suite at an exclusive hotel. The corridors are sealed. The guests are questioned. The world waits for a simple explanation.

But behind polished smiles and carefully rehearsed statements, a web of rivalries, private loyalties, and inexplicable events begins to surface. The closer you get to the truth, the less certain reality becomes.
